simulate chess games using the maia 3 chess engine.

great for studying openings, and thematic middle games, or just positions you don't understand,
especially when there are not many Master or online games.

you can configure elo and playing style (you can choose from :SOLID/TEXTBOOK, NATURAL, GAMBITTER, BULLET, BLUNDERING)
which are templates for a comination of temperature and top-p.
you can also instead use a custom combination of your own.

you can copy the PGN for each game generated.
